01 — ABOUT NEXUS
Where NEXUS Came From

The Origin Story

NEXUS was built during the Gurugram Police Cyber Security Summer Internship (GPCSSI) 2026 — a month-long internship where every intern was given the full duration, start to finish, to design and build one project of their own choosing.

Cyber awareness was chosen as the focus — because most breaches don't start with sophisticated hacking, they start with a person who didn't know what to look for. NEXUS is the result: a free, hands-on way for anyone to learn that in minutes, not months.

What Is GPCSSI?

GPCSSI — the Gurugram Police Cyber Security Summer Internship — is a program run by Gurugram Police that gives students hands-on exposure to real cybersecurity work and challenges them to build something useful during the internship period.

NEXUS is that project — a cyber awareness platform combining working security tools, interactive quizzes, and hands-on games, built solo across the internship and shipped as a free web app.
